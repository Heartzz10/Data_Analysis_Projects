# Data Analysis Projects: Iris, Regional Livability, and Urban Perception

🙌Here, I present my three insightful **data analysis projects** that utilize **Pandas** and **NumPy** to explore data, extract meaningful insights, and address real-world challenges. These projects demonstrate diverse applications of data analysis, from natural datasets to urban livability and perception studies.😊

---

## Projects Overview

### 1. **Analyzing Iris Flower Species Data**
- **Objective**: Explore the Iris dataset to understand the distinguishing characteristics of three flower species (setosa, versicolor, virginica).  
- **Experiment Workflow**:  
  - Load and inspect the Iris dataset.  
  - Perform feature-wise statistical analysis and generate descriptive summaries.  
  - Use visualizations to examine feature distributions and relationships across species.  
  - Derive meaningful insights for further classification tasks.  
- **Key Findings**:  
  - Clear separability in feature space between species.  
  - Strong correlations observed between petal and sepal measurements, which are critical in differentiating the species.

---

### 2. **Scoring Regional Livability**
- **Objective**: Develop a scoring system to evaluate and compare the livability of different regions based on multiple factors.  
- **Experiment Workflow**:  
  - Define factors affecting livability, such as rental prices, access to amenities, noise levels, and environmental metrics.  
  - Preprocess and normalize the data for consistency across metrics.  
  - Use the **Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP)** to determine the weighted importance of each factor.  
  - Aggregate the weighted scores into a comprehensive livability index.  
- **Key Findings**:  
  - Produced a detailed ranking of regional livability, enabling easy comparison.  
  - Highlighted key strengths and weaknesses for each region, offering actionable insights for improvement.

---

### 3. **Analysis of Factors Influencing Urban Perception Scores**
- **Objective**: Identify and analyze the most influential factors shaping urban perception scores.  
- **Experiment Workflow**:  
  - Collect and clean data related to urban perception (e.g., population density, accessibility of services, noise levels).  
  - Engineer features for deeper insights, combining related metrics into new variables.  
  - Perform exploratory analysis to uncover patterns and correlations between factors and perception scores.  
  - Validate findings with visualizations and statistical evidence.  
- **Key Findings**:  
  - Identified key positive and negative contributors to urban perception scores.  
  - Provided actionable recommendations for improving urban environments based on data-driven insights.
